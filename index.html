PatilWagh Video Automation

Good vibes only — peaceful, positive, uplifting.

PatilWagh Video Automation is a private tool used only by the owner of the PatilWagh-GoodVibesOnly YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Prepares short motivational videos (40–60 seconds) in English, Hindi and Marathi, using original writing and properly licensed footage, music and fonts.
  • Prepares titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails for each video.
  • Keeps a record of licences for every asset used.
  • Presents each video to the channel owner for review. Nothing is uploaded or scheduled without the owner's explicit approval.
  • After approval, uploads the video to the owner's channel using the official YouTube Data API and schedules it for publication.

Google and YouTube access

The tool signs in with the channel owner's own Google account through Google's official OAuth sign-in. It uses YouTube API Services. By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See our Privacy Policy for details of the data it uses, and our Terms of Service.
